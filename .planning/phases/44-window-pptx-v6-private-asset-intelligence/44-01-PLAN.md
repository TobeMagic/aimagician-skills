# Phase 44: Private Asset Intelligence - Plan 01

**Created:** 2026-07-30
**Requirements:** V6R-MINE-01
**Requests:** USR-V6-11, USR-V6-12
**Status:** Approved

## Objective

Convert every safely rendered private page into an exact, independently
reviewed complete-layout, support/specialty, or excluded disposition and
certify only distinct high-quality pages with full evidence.

## Non-Goals

- No production deck materialization.
- No commercial-byte redistribution or credential/source disclosure.
- No page-count inflation by restoring denied content.

## Tasks

**Requirements:** V6R-MINE-01
**Depends on:** Phase 43
**Slice:** Tracer
**Allowed files:** Phase 44 planning, private-intelligence/contact-sheet code,
CLIs, disposition registry, focused tests.
**Forbidden files:** tracked private bytes, credentials, source URLs.

2. Render every accepted page and bind PNG evidence to package/slide hashes.

**Checkpoint:** private asset-index aggregate counts and focused tests.

**Requirements:** V6R-MINE-01

**Slice:** Boundary

### Task 3: Cross-pool dedupe and evidence-complete v2 certification

**Requirements:** V6R-MINE-01

**Acceptance:** GOAL-44-03 and GOAL-44-04 pass.
**Integration points:** Phase 45 selection/materialization input.
