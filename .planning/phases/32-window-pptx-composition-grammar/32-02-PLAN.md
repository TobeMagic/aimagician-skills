# Phase 32 Plan 02: CompositionPlan, Agnes, and Reference-Grade R3–R6

**Created:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** Implementation complete / visual acceptance `NO_GO`
**Requirements:** P32-COMP-01, P32-DESIGN-01, P32-NARRATIVE-01,
P32-ASSET-01, P32-AGNES-01, P32-QA-01, P32-REPAIR-01

## Objective

Materialize the accepted consulting art direction through a validated
CompositionPlan, direct Agnes providers, Quality v3, and bounded
composition-level repair, then produce real R3–R6 evidence without claiming
reference-grade release.

## Non-Goals

- Multi-scenario Phase 33 expansion, formal Phase 35 weak-model benchmark,
  COM repair, or HTML-to-PPTX.

## Rollback And Recovery

- Contracts are additive; compatibility callers keep the old seed/v2 path.
- Provider or generated-asset failure uses deterministic native fallback.
- Rejected repair preserves the last monotonic candidate and protected
  content digest.
- R3/R4 evidence is versioned separately; never overwrite R2 or the reference.
- Do not reset, clean, or discard unrelated user work.
