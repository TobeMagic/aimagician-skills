## Visual iteration record

The first custom work-report anchor was below the supplied reference. It was
replaced with exact authorized TemplatePack materialization. Campus evolved
from an all-dark card/dashboard grammar to a deliberate daylight/dark rhythm
with custom chapter diagrams, market path, and stakeholder columns. Academic
gained a dark readable agenda, native method diagrams, a clearer evidence
grid, simplified multiscale page, and finally a method-integrated bespoke
cover.

The first four-image blind packet also exposed reviewer cross-candidate
confusion and thumbnail-resolution noise. The final protocol uses one
candidate per isolated context and two half-deck contact sheets. This is both
more blind and more reliable: each reviewer can inspect every page, cannot
confuse candidate IDs, and remains unaware of implementation.

Final pairwise AI-only blind verdicts are 4.75, 4.55, and 4.45, all
reference-parity PASS with zero Blocker or Important.

The fresh independent OpenCode completion audit also returned `DONE` with all
four goals PASS, zero Blocker, zero Important, and an unchanged frozen
worktree fingerprint. See `46-COMPLETION-AUDIT.md`.
