without discarding the useful portable v5.1 foundation.

## Options

| Option | Benefits | Costs and risks | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continue expanding deterministic cards and repair rules | Reuses v5 code directly | Repeats rejected visual language and repair complexity | Already falsified by user review |
| Let GPT-5.5 generate unrestricted layouts/code | Broad creative search | Fact, safety, editability, reproducibility, and compatibility risk | Rejected by accepted constraints |
| Locked briefs + certified complete-work spines + constrained GPT-5.5 selection | Strong content truth, real art direction, editable output, bounded model authority | Requires schemas, corpus, catalog, rights, and materializer work | Three flagship tracer and blind parity review |

## Recommendation

Use the third option. Phase 36 first locks content truth and private safety.
Phases 37–38 build the catalog and template intelligence. Phases 39–40 deliver
the three flagships. Phase 41 owns independent AI acceptance.
