# Phase 36: v6 Contracts and Realistic Corpus - Plan 01

**Created:** 2026-07-29
**Requirements:** V6-BRIEF-01, V6-CORPUS-01, V6-DOC-01
**Status:** Accepted

## Objective

Establish the safe, executable v6 intake boundary: private commercial assets
cannot enter git, formal authoring cannot start before a realistic brief is
discussion-locked, and fifteen scenario packs provide sufficient facts,
materials, anatomy, and acceptance criteria for reference-grade deck work.

## Non-Goals

- Do not acquire or commit commercial template binaries or credentials.
- Do not implement TemplatePack v2, Registry v3, or final flagship decks.
- Do not weaken Quality v3 or silently accept the three archived regressions.
- Do not make COM, HTML conversion, human scoring, or a weak model the v6
  canonical path.
