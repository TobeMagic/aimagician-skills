# Phase 38 Research

## Objective

Identify the smallest executable architecture that converts the certified
catalog and authorized reference into bounded complete-deck visual selection
without weakening v1 compatibility or waiting for commercial authentication.

## Local Evidence

The evidence set is the existing registries and loaders, the authorized
physical TemplatePack, the exact rendered reference, Phase 37 catalog
contracts, and the locked v6 flagship briefs.

## Current Architecture Finding

The current library is not one runtime system. Catalog v3, layout variants,
ArtDirectionProfile v1, DesignPack, the legacy XLSX/registry, and physical
TemplatePack v1 are independently valid but generation does not consume a
single certified selection plan.

The highest-leverage change is therefore a compatibility facade and selection
contract, not another large collection of decorative components.

## Visual Findings

The reference derives quality from deck-level choreography more than from any
single component:

- six sparse typographic bookend/reset pages divide nine denser evidence
  pages;
- one motif family changes crop, scale, transparency, and role across cover,
  directory, section, body, and closing;
- evidence pages deliberately change form instead of repeating a generic
  card grid;
- theme colors and native chart/table language bind diverse page forms into
  one visual system.

The implementation should retain those mechanisms while improving small
labels, 3D chart effects, icon-language mixing, and decorative collisions.

## Prior Architecture Review Incorporated

The completed template-library architecture review established:

- complete works should be visual spines;
- supplemental pages stay inside the same deck/style family;
- stable page identity, capacity, semantic metadata, dependency closure, and
  typed slots are required;
- GPT-5.5 medium should choose candidates, not draw coordinates;
- HTML-first loses native PowerPoint semantics and remains proof-only;
- physical OOXML adaptation and registered native composition are the two safe
  pilot materializers.

## Options

1. Index all 110 variants directly: broad, but fails to distinguish
   certification, specialist grammar, and complete-work context.
2. Make the model draw pages: flexible, but violates the accepted bounded
   authority and weakens repeatability.
3. Add a digest-bound facade with complete-work spines and certified
   candidates: additive, testable, and compatible.

## Recommendation

Use an additive `template_intelligence.py` owner with strict schemas and a
small static registry policy. Derive the pilot deterministically from the
existing layout registry rather than duplicating 84 large geometry objects.
Bind each candidate to a known recipe and materializer. Store complete-work
art direction and choreography in three v2 spine manifests. Keep TemplatePack
v1 physical adaptation unchanged and expose it through a v2 adapter.

This provides an executable vertical tracer for Phase 39 without pretending
that commercial inventory or final visual parity already exists.

## Assumptions To Confirm

- The packaged authorized reference remains byte-identical to its manifest.
- The governed 25 layout families retain at least two executable variants and
  ten families retain a third supplement.
- Registered composition remains capable of materializing every certified
  alias without raw model geometry.
