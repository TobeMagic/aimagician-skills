# Phase 38 Specification: Certified Template Intelligence

**Status:** Locked
**Depends on:** Phase 37 `SEED_READY`; full sync may continue
**Requirements:** V6-LIB-01, V6-DESIGN-01, V6-DECK-01

## Goal

Introduce TemplatePack v2, Registry v3, ArtDirectionProfile, semantic
retrieval, and same-family materialization while retaining v5 compatibility.

## Acceptance

- Registry v3 wraps every current registry through an expand-contract facade;
  old public fields and selection behavior remain compatible.
- The certified pilot contains 60–100 independent page candidates plus three
  licensed complete-work spines.
- Complete works own visual spine, rhythm, motif, theme, grid, typography,
  imagery, and chart/table language.
- SmartArt, macros, OLE, and ActiveX are preserve-only or blocked; nothing is
  silently rasterized.
