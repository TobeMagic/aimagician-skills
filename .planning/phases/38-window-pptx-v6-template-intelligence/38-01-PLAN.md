**Created:** 2026-07-30
**Requirements:** V6-LIB-01, V6-DESIGN-01, V6-DECK-01
**Status:** Accepted

## Objective

Deliver an additive, executable TemplatePack v2 and Registry v3 tracer that
turns a locked flagship brief into a certified complete-work spine,
TemplateSelectionPlan, and validated SlideBlueprint sequence.

## Non-Goals

- Do not download commercial templates or read `.private/`.
- Do not weaken certification, rights, capacity, or legacy quarantine.
- Do not replace TemplatePack v1 or existing registries.
- Do not implement arbitrary cross-source OOXML slide import.
- Do not claim final flagship visual GO before Phases 39–41.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Lock visual and contract evidence

**Requirements:** V6-DESIGN-01, V6-DECK-01

Render the exact authorized reference, record its package inventory and
digest, obtain an independent Agnes visual inspection, and encode the
reusable hierarchy/rhythm/motif/page-family rules plus anti-patterns.

**Acceptance:** the evidence is exact-source bound; visual claims cite slide
regions; no protected identity or unlicensed asset is generalized as a
required token.

### Task 2: TemplatePack v2 and ArtDirectionProfile v2

**Requirements:** V6-LIB-01, V6-DECK-01

Add strict schemas and loaders for complete-work source mode, rights,
certification, art direction, choreography, candidate pages, typed slots,
capacity, dependencies, alternatives, materializer, compatibility, and
portable proof. Adapt TemplatePack v1 without changing its public behavior.

**Acceptance:** the existing physical pack adapts losslessly; unknown fields,
bad digests, unsupported active content, missing materializers, and incomplete
spines fail closed.

### Task 3: Registry v3 and certified 84-candidate pilot

**Requirements:** V6-LIB-01

Add a digest-bound expand-contract facade and derive a balanced 84-candidate
pilot as 15 authorized physical pages, 60 governed registered-composition
variants, and nine explicit specialty aliases with registered materializers.
Preserve every registered page family, map the user taxonomy, and register the
physical work-report plus first-party campus and academic spines.

**Acceptance:** exactly 84 certified candidates; every family and taxonomy
need is covered; legacy/unverified items never auto-select; v1 loader
regressions pass.

### Task 4: Semantic retrieval, selection plan, and blueprints

**Requirements:** V6-DESIGN-01, V6-DECK-01

Implement hard filters, deterministic hybrid ranking, diversity selection,
spine choice, candidate explanations, strict model-output validation,
capacity/asset/fact/dependency/family checks, mandatory anatomy expansion,
and deck-level rhythm validation.

**Acceptance:** a locked flagship brief produces a stable plan; shuffled
registry order does not change it; forbidden raw design/code fields, unknown
IDs, unbound facts, missing assets, over-capacity content, and style drift fail
closed; a same-family alternative is the only local fallback.

### Task 5: Skill integration and phase audit

**Requirements:** V6-LIB-01, V6-DESIGN-01, V6-DECK-01

Document the new authoring seam, add behavior evals and focused tests, run
affected/full regressions, workflow/formatter/private/diff gates, and obtain
fresh independent specification, quality, verification, visual, and
completion reviews.

**Acceptance:** all gates pass and no reviewer returns an unresolved Blocker
or Important. Phase closure explicitly states that final flagship parity is
still owned by Phases 39–41.
