# Phase 40 Research

## Objective

Translate two very different realistic briefs into complete, editable decks
without falling back to a generic card template.

## Local Evidence

- Locked campus facts: 18 nodes, 84 days, 214,680 valid records, 98.6%
  completeness, precision/recall/F1, 42-minute lead time, cost/market bounds.
- Locked academic facts: two datasets, three horizons, complete baselines,
  ablation, robustness, repeated runs, parameters, latency, limitations, and
  citation boundaries.

## Options

- One shared business-report theme: rejected as semantically weak.
- Image-heavy concept decks: rejected because required assets are synthetic and
  native editability is mandatory.
- Separate certified spines with reusable primitives and scenario-specific
  compositions: chosen.

## Recommendation

Campus should use a signal-map, dashboard mockup, architecture, pilot charts,
business model, risk matrix, and varied Q&A layouts. Academic should use
network diagrams, dynamic-graph and multiscale diagrams, comparison charts,
ablation, robustness, efficiency, limitations, and complete appendix tables.

## Assumptions To Confirm

- Campus coordinates and UI are governed illustrations, not deployment claims.
- Academic numerical results are synthetic evaluation logs.
- The appendix may be denser than the main deck but must remain readable.
