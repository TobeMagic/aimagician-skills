# Phase 40 Context

## Implementation Decisions

- Campus and academic tracers consume only their locked ProjectBriefPacks.
- Campus uses the `product-launch-stage` spine with navy/teal signal language,
  native map/product/business-model visuals, 22 main slides, 4 appendices, and
  6 Q&A backups.
- Academic uses `data-research-editorial` with ivory/navy/coral evidence
  language, 26 main slides, and 6 appendices.
- Section transitions remain inside each deck's base palette; diagrams and
  charts use semantic, native-editable forms.

## Existing Patterns To Preserve

- Locked realistic briefs, fact IDs, source boundaries, and notes lineage.
- Certified scenario spine selection and registered native materializers.
- Portable isolated rendering and physical-page evidence.
- Fail-closed unsupported-claim and whole-slide-raster guards.

## Allowed Scope

- The v6 flagship generator, focused tests, Phase 40 evidence/planning, and
  native shapes/charts/tables/notes required by the two locked briefs.

## Forbidden Scope

- Unsupported SOTA, causality, deployment, market, order, patent, award, or
  commercial-scale claims.
- Screenshot mockups presented as real products, invented campus coordinates,
  whole-slide rasterization, mandatory COM, and private assets.

## Integration And Compatibility

Both tracers use the same TemplatePack v2/Registry v3 certification and
portable evidence contracts as Phase 39. They add scenario-specific
compositions without changing legacy public render APIs.
