fixtures, then run one external clean-room Codex work-report acceptance and

  model-written geometry/style/code.

## Rollback And Recovery

- Revert only the scoped stabilization commit; the v6.1 checkpoint remains
  available on `feat/window-pptx-v6`.
- Never mutate or delete private source packages during repair.
- Failed clean-room or review evidence keeps the phase active and resumes from
  the last frozen passing engineering checkpoint.
- The v4 library index is regenerated deterministically after the new record
  contract; old records missing direct-use disposition fail closed and are
  never silently treated as eligible.
- Before promotion, record the prior source SHA, installed Skill digest, and
  library-index digest. A postmerge failure restores or rolls forward source,
  installed Skill, and regenerated index together, then reruns parity plus the
  narrow compile/query/assembly smoke before a new completion audit.
