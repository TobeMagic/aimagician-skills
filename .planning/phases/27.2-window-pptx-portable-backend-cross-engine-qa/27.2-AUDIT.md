**Status:** Complete

## Audit Questions

1. Does governed `auto` delivery complete with COM denied and without starting PowerPoint?
2. Does capability negotiation reject unsupported required behavior before mutation?
3. Are all promised portable objects native/editable and identity-bound rather than slide screenshots?
4. Are normalized packages byte-deterministic under one complete fingerprint?
5. Does OOXML inspection compare the generated package to RenderPlan rather than merely open the ZIP?
6. Does LibreOffice operate on a copy with an isolated profile, and does its real output feed Quality-v2?
7. Can HTML proof fail without affecting the PPTX or delivery result?
8. Is PowerPoint certification optional, read-only, macro-disabled, process-owned, and registry-non-mutating?
9. Are legacy COM capabilities preserved without becoming an implicit fallback?
10. Do six real calibration packets and full regression evidence support every V5-PORT requirement?

## Known Risks To Reassess

- Overclaiming native groups, motion, template import, or renderer equivalence.
- Reusing pre-27.2 manifests or the pre-implementation DeepSeek audit as release evidence.
