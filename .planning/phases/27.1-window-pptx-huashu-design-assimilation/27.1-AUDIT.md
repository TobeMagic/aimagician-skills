## Audit Summary

Before Phase 27.1, Window-PPTX already had a strong engineering base: strict DeckPlan validation, 15 business archetypes, semantic form ranking, 8 themes, 24 page families/72 variants, native editable objects, transactional saving, five-layer quality gates, and bounded post-render repair. Its principal weakness was upstream authority: the model still authored factual text, story structure, design direction, and many layout choices directly in DeckPlan.

The implementation materially lowers that dependency. In the default weak-model route, the model can only group immutable fact IDs and select registered beat/semantic hints. Deterministic code now owns narrative completion, action-title construction, theme/art direction, layout variants, asset fallback, quality classification, and repair. This is a real architectural improvement, not only additional prompt guidance.

The audit cannot conclude that ordinary models now match a senior PPT designer. The local evidence uses Recording COM rather than real saved PPTX, both frozen OpenCode providers failed, the formal 180-trial benchmark was not executed, and no blind human visual review exists.

## Baseline Capability Assessment

The pre-enhancement Skill already provided:

- Strict DeckPlan v1 with raw coordinate/font/color rejection.
- Fifteen commercial archetypes and deterministic semantic-to-page ranking.
- Capacity-aware splitting, sparse-page handling, and page-family rhythm checks.
- Eight themes, 24 page families, 72 layout IDs, native editable text/shapes/images/charts/tables/diagrams, notes, links, and controlled motion.
- Transactional PowerPoint save/export, source protection, package validation, editability checks, and quality v1 repair.

The baseline still depended heavily on model capability for:

- Choosing and completing the commercial story.
- Preserving all supplied facts without copying errors or unsupported numbers.
- Producing action-oriented titles and sensible information grouping.
- Establishing an art direction and matching it consistently across pages.
- Knowing when assets were insufficient before selecting image-dependent layouts.
- Avoiding repetitive cards, decorative noise, concept/content mismatch, and near-duplicate pages.

## Baseline Failure Modes

Ordinary-model failures most likely occurred at these boundaries:

1. Malformed or near-valid JSON, aliases, invalid IDs, unknown keys, and unsupported raw design fields.
2. Missing, paraphrased, duplicated, or invented facts and quantities.
3. Incomplete critical narrative beats or direct text-to-template dumping.
4. One repeated layout family, excessive cards, weak hierarchy, or arbitrary visual changes.
5. Image layouts selected without valid assets, producing placeholders or weak empty regions.
6. Chart choice not matching the selected semantic relationship.
7. Quality checks occurring too late to safely replan story/design decisions.

## Enhanced Architecture

1. Trusted project inputs become a strict immutable FactStore with source IDs, locators, optional hashes, fact states, and conflict detection.
2. The model returns BriefPlan only: scenario ID, unique fact references, registered beat/semantic hints, importance, and bounded preferences.
3. NarrativePlan assigns missing required facts safely, requires registered critical beats, orders by the archetype, creates structural cover/agenda/closing pages, and preserves factual text exactly.
4. Canonical DeckPlan remains the compatibility boundary for all existing compiler/render code.
5. Direction selection produces deterministic safe/editorial/expressive candidates from 12 neutral profiles, applies brand/asset/font gates, and chooses governed themes/layout preferences.
6. RenderPlan uses native editable PowerPoint primitives; unusable assets are rejected before layout, passive provenance-bound SVG logos/icons/vectors are accepted, XML base/processing instructions/CSS escapes/SMIL animation are rejected, and missing image semantics fall back to native statements.
7. Quality v2 merges input, narrative, compile, render, preview, package, and editability findings. One pre-render and one post-render pass may be accepted only on lexicographic improvement without canonical protected-content change; exceptions or regressions roll back. Expected PNG previews are a pre-save hard gate.
8. The transaction specifies the requested OOXML format, reopens the saved candidate, and repeats semantic/editability checks before atomic promotion. `reopened-content-validation` is mandatory and only orchestration-observed callback evidence counts. Preflight artifacts are persisted before COM startup so Windows failures do not erase model-independent evidence.

## Template, Component, and Design Systems

- All existing 24 page families retain at least three deterministic geometry variants; art direction influences ranking only after semantic compatibility.
- Twelve neutral profiles cover safe, editorial, evidence-dense, diagrammatic, keynote, asymmetric, and typography-led modes without copying Huashu named styles.
- BrandSpec constrains all six registered semantic colors, fonts, required assets, and prohibited patterns. Required brand assets/fonts and fidelity-breaking theme fallbacks are hard gates; generation evidence binds normalized BrandSpec content/source/hash, sorted font inventory/digest, normalized asset bindings/source/hash, and all theme resolution events.
- Existing theme tokens continue to govern type, color, spacing, margins, effects, chart, table, and native component implementation.
- Direction proof slide IDs are deterministic and interactive mode emits proof RenderPlan data before COM.

## Content-to-Layout Rules

- Trend semantics drive line/area-compatible native charts.
- Composition semantics drive stacked or doughnut-compatible native charts.
- Comparison, sequence/process, timeline, roadmap, quadrant, funnel, matrix, risk, recommendation, table, image, and statement semantics are compiled through registered page families and capacity filters.
- Critical archetype beats must have fact-backed groups; the compiler does not invent filler pages to reach a target slide count.
- Layout preference is a small deterministic tie-breaker, not permission to select a semantically incompatible family.
- Repeated family runs, card monoculture, direction drift, content/concept mismatch, excessive decoration, density, whitespace, edge crop, and near-duplicate previews produce findings.

## Quality and Repair Assessment

Strengths:

- Stable namespace/severity ordering and deduplication.
- Immutable fact digest across repair.
- Maximum one pre-render and one post-render mutation attempt for the new route.
- Snapshot rollback on exception, regression, or invariant violation.
- PNG preview inspection fails closed on the production Brief route when expected exports are missing, unreadable, or unavailable.
- Native editability remains the default; fallback uses editable shapes/text rather than placeholder raster content.
- Candidate reopen validation repeats text, tag, chart, table, diagram, and editability coverage checks after PowerPoint serialization and before promotion.
- Benchmark blind scoring fails closed until one verified readable OOXML deck plus verified slide PNGs are staged under anonymized review paths.

Limitations:

- Preview heuristics cannot replace real visual judgment and may produce false positives on intentional edge-to-edge imagery or dense research pages.
- Anti-slop/content-fit findings are important findings, not universal delivery hard gates.
- The expanded benchmark scenarios now carry 5-8 trusted facts and the six calibration decks reach their governed 8-10 slide ranges without filler. Delivery-readiness interpretation still requires real ordinary-model responses and rendered evidence.
- The calibration does not render actual fonts, crops, chart labels, or PowerPoint line breaks.

## Huashu 1+1>2 Assessment

The useful combination is methodological: Huashu's story-first, multiple-direction, proof-page, asset-first, and anti-slop ideas are converted into strict native-PPTX registries and gates. Window-PPTX contributes fact authority, native editability, transactional saving, deterministic layout, and machine-verifiable QA that Huashu's HTML/prompt workflow does not provide.

The historical combination avoided a second renderer and avoided copied styles/assets. Phase 27.2 supersedes only the first choice: an independently implemented LibreOffice proof path is now required to prevent the generator from validating its own output. The result remains provisional until real portable artifacts, ordinary-model results, sampled PowerPoint certification, and human-review evidence exist.
