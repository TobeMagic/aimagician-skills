# Phase 41 Research

## Objective

Establish a reliable AI-only release gate that prevents reference/candidate
image confusion and rejects unsupported visual findings.

## Local Evidence

Early Direct Agnes rounds misattributed reference slides to candidates.
Physical banners and segmented candidate contact sheets fixed attachment
identity, while a Pillow lifecycle repair fixed evidence-generation
termination. Failed rounds remain preserved as protocol diagnostics.

## Options

- One AI score: rejected as fragile.
- Three continued conversations: rejected because context leakage violates the
  blind contract.
- Three independent visual contexts with identical frozen rubric: chosen.

## Recommendation

Require full image-load preflight, anonymous hashes, exact nine-dimension
scores, at least two of three parity decisions, overall mean >=4.3, dimension
means >=4.1, each flagship >=4.2, and no two reviewers agreeing on a same-slide
Blocker/Important issue.

## Assumptions To Confirm

- Visible semantic coherence can be scored; OOXML editability is verified
  separately.
- Reference parity means comparable professional finish, not visual copying.
