## Runtime SKILL.md

Keep the runtime file progressively loadable:

1. narrow description with named-target and task triggers plus non-trigger;
2. transparent perspective-model disclaimer;
3. question routing: factual, framework, mixed, unsafe;
4. model-selection procedure;
5. 3-7 mental models with evidence IDs and failure conditions;
6. 5-10 decision heuristics;
7. expression constraints and anti-caricature rules;
8. values, anti-patterns, tensions, and changes over time;
9. honest boundaries, cutoff date, and evidence routing;
10. output structure for advice, analysis, or critique.

Heavy research remains under `references/`. Do not put biography, promotional copy, creator attribution, or installation instructions in the runtime Skill.

## Factual Question Protocol

When current facts materially affect the answer:

1. identify the facts the perspective would inspect;
2. obtain current, attributable evidence;
3. separate facts from perspective interpretation;
4. apply only the relevant mental models;
5. state where the actual person's view is unknown.

## Fidelity Record

`FIDELITY.md` records:

- research cutoff;
- coverage by evidence stream;
- primary/secondary ratios;
- model-to-evidence mapping;
- known-position, edge, voice, contradiction, and non-trigger results;
- weak dimensions and unsupported areas;
- update history.
