# Research And Synthesis

## Evidence Streams

| Stream | Look for | Persist |
|---|---|---|
| Authored work | recurring arguments, named concepts, long-form reasoning | `01-writings.md` |
| Conversations | reasoning under challenge, analogies, changed answers, refusals | `02-conversations.md` |
| Expression | sentence patterns, vocabulary, certainty, humor, pacing | `03-expression-dna.md` |
| External views | criticism, comparison, observed blind spots | `04-external-views.md` |
| Decisions | context, incentives, action, result, retrospective account | `05-decisions.md` |
| Timeline | milestones, transitions, current cutoff | `06-timeline.md` |

Each claim records URL or local path, date, primary/secondary status, direct evidence or inference, and confidence. Preserve contradictory evidence.

## Source Priority

1. authorized local primary material;
2. authored books, essays, talks, and original transcripts;
3. documented decisions and contemporaneous records;
4. high-quality interviews;
5. external criticism and biography;
6. unverifiable summaries.

Do not use inaccessible snippets as if the full source was read. Quotations require a verifiable source.

## Mental-Model Tests

A mental model passes only when:

- it recurs across at least two domains or decision types;
- it generates a defensible prediction for a new problem;
- it is distinctive enough to change an answer.

A partial pass becomes a heuristic or hypothesis. Zero-pass content is excluded.

## Expression DNA

Measure representative long and short samples:

- sentence length and question ratio;
- analogy density and concrete/abstract balance;
- first-person and certainty language;
- conclusion-first versus buildup;
- humor, rhythm, and citation habits;
- words or behaviors the target consistently avoids.

Use style as a constraint, not a caricature. Never fabricate signature phrases.

## Contradictions

Classify:

- temporal change;
- domain-specific rule;
- unresolved value tension;
- evidence conflict.

The generated Skill selects the relevant dated or domain-specific view and states uncertainty instead of synthesizing a false compromise.

## Topic Variant

For a topic rather than one person:

- identify 3-5 major schools or contributors;
- extract shared frameworks and explicit disagreements;
- use neutral professional expression;
- validate against canonical cases rather than one person's known position.
