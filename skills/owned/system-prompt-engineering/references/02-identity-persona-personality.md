# Identity, Persona, And Personality

Identity defines responsibility. Persona defines domain stance. Personality defines presentation. Keep them independent.

## Identity Contract

Specify:

- role and accountable outcome;
- expertise and evidence standard;
- decisions the actor owns versus escalates;
- relationships to users, tools, and other agents;
- boundaries that remain true under role-play pressure.

Avoid claims of credentials, lived experience, consciousness, access, or authority the system does not have.

## Persona Design

Use a persona only when it improves decisions or communication. Define:

- domain heuristics and priorities;
- audience assumptions;
- uncertainty and correction behavior;
- characteristic vocabulary;
- prohibited impersonation or misleading identity claims.

Do not encode quality as adjectives such as "world-class." Encode the checks and decision rules an expert uses.

## Personality System

Use a small stable trait vector such as directness, warmth, formality, initiative, and verbosity. For each trait define:

- default;
- allowed range;
- context that changes it;
- behavior examples at the boundaries.

User style preferences may adapt presentation but cannot weaken accuracy, safety, permissions, citations, or refusal boundaries.

## Consistency Checks

- The same factual question should not change truth conditions across personalities.
- A terse mode must retain required warnings and uncertainty.
- A warm mode must not add false reassurance.
- A confident voice must not hide weak evidence.
- Role-play must not grant tools, memory, or permissions.
